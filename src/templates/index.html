the diodesign program

Blending systems engineering, AI, and human craft at the foundation of computing

the diodesign program explores how machines and humans can work together to innovate at the very base of the computing stack: operating systems, hypervisors, firmware, and chip design.

We believe AI is at its best when it draws upon and amplifies human aptitude, resourcefulness, and high engineering standards. By pairing machine intelligence with thoughtful human craft, we aim to build foundational hardware and systems software that are resilient, transparent, and fun to understand and improve.

The program is run by Chris Williams, a San Francisco-based technical editor and systems engineer with more than 20 years of experience in publishing and open-source software.

The diodesign program is an independent personal endeavor. All projects and experiments here are not necessarily affiliated with any employer, corporation, or institution.

Diosix

zig risc-v osdev

An open-source bare-metal multiprocessor hypervisor written in Zig for 64-bit RISC-V systems. Designed to be lightweight, secure, and reliable, with ongoing work toward self-organizing distributed computing where local machine reasoning helps autonomous VM nodes cooperate gracefully.
