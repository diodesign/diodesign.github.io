the diodesign lab

Synthesizing silicon and intelligence through low-level systems experimentation

Here in the diodesign lab, we wander off the beaten track to see what happens when we mix low-level systems programming, self-organizing architectures, and a little too much curiosity. When those experiments stabilize, we release them into the wild as open-source software for others to use or build upon.

The lab is run by Chris Williams, a San Francisco-based technical editor and systems engineer with more than 20 years of experience in technology publishing and open-source software, including past roles as editor-in-chief of The Register and editor of the Arm-systems site Drobe.

The diodesign lab is an independent personal endeavor. All projects and experiments here are strictly personal and are not affiliated with any employer, corporation, or institution.

Diosix

Zig · RISC-V · Hypervisor

An open-source bare-metal multiprocessor hypervisor written in Zig for 64-bit RISC-V systems. Designed to be lightweight, secure, and reliable, with ongoing work toward decentralized, self-organizing distributed computing across autonomous VM meshes.
