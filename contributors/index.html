Contributing to the lab

We push boundaries through collaboration. If you want to help out, we'd love to hear from you.

Website and CMS

If you'd like to contribute to the lab website or its build system, send an email to Chris. Our licensing terms govern all contributions to the website. While you retain copyright of your work, we share your contributions under these terms to keep the lab open for everyone.

Lab projects

To contribute to an existing project, refer to that project's contribution guidelines and licensing. If you have an idea for a new project, send an email to Chris with your proposal.
