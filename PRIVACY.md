# Privacy policy

[diodesign.org](https://diodesign.org/) is a static single-page website for **the diodesign program** with no backend server, database, or third-party tracking scripts.

The site's maintainer does not collect, log, store, or sell any personal data from visitors.

- **No cookies or client storage:** The site does not set cookies or use browser storage.
- **No analytics or tracking:** The site contains no tracking pixels, telemetry, advertising, or analytics scripts.
- **Local in-browser execution:** All interactive features—including the expandable panels, procedural landscape, and RISC-V instruction explorer—run entirely within your browser and transmit no data over the network.

## How and when user information is disclosed to third parties

Although the diodesign program does not collect visitor data, loading the website or following its links involves third-party services that receive standard connection information from your browser.

### Automatic disclosures upon page load

When you visit diodesign.org, your browser automatically connects to two third-party services:

- **GitHub Pages:** The site and its source repository are hosted on GitHub and served by GitHub Pages. When your browser requests the page and its static assets, GitHub receives standard HTTP and network metadata—such as your IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL, timestamp, and referring page—to deliver content and maintain security. See the [GitHub general privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [GitHub Pages data collection notice](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages#data-collection).
- **Google Fonts:** The site loads the Outfit font family from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches the font stylesheet and font files from Google's servers, Google receives your IP address, user-agent, language preferences, and the referring site address. See the [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Fonts privacy FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).

### User-initiated disclosures

No other third party receives information about your visit unless you choose to interact with an external link or contact channel:

- **External links:** The site links to external websites and platforms, including GitHub, Bluesky, and LinkedIn. Clicking an external link directs your browser to that destination, which receives standard connection data under its own privacy policy.
- **Direct correspondence:** If you send an email to the diodesign program or reach out on an external platform, your contact details and message are processed by the respective email or messaging providers. Messages sent directly to the program are used only to respond to your inquiry and are never sold or shared unless required by law.
- **GitHub repository interactions:** If you view, star, fork, or contribute to the [source repository on GitHub](https://github.com/diodesign/diodesign.github.io), your activity and public profile information are processed by GitHub under its platform terms.
